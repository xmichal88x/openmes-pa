# Trademark and branding policy

This policy covers the **OpenMES PA** fork in this repository. It does not grant
any rights to the upstream OpenMES name, logo, or other marks, which remain the
property of their respective owners.

## Allowed without permission

- Nominative, factual references such as "based on OpenMES v0.28.0" or
  "OpenMES PA, a modified OpenMES distribution".
- Keeping the upstream `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, and attribution notices intact, as
  the licenses require.
- Linking to this repository or to the upstream project.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Using the OpenMES name, logo, or brand assets in a way that suggests this fork
  is the official OpenMES project, or that its authors endorse your deployment.
- Removing or altering copyright, license, or attribution notices.
- Distributing a modified build under the unqualified name "OpenMES" or with the
  upstream logo as the primary product branding.

## Modified distributions

If you redistribute a modified version of OpenMES PA:

1. Keep `LICENSE`, `LICENSING.md`, `NOTICE`, and this file with the distribution.
2. Mark your changes and their date, as required by AGPL §5.
3. Use your own name and branding for the modified product, and describe the
   relationship factually (for example, "a fork of OpenMES PA").
4. Do not imply endorsement by edeseczka or by the upstream OpenMES project.

## Questions

Open an issue in https://github.com/xmichal88x/openmes-pa for clarification
before using the marks in promotional material.
